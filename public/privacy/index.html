Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026.

This website sets no cookies, has no sign-up, and does not collect personal information.

The Whisper Almanac Uploader is a private tool used only by Whisper Almanac to publish videos to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It signs in to that one channel only. It does not access, store or share data from any other person's Google or YouTube account.

  • What it uses: permission to upload and manage videos, thumbnails, captions and channel details on the Whisper Almanac channel, and to read that channel's own view counts.
  • What it stores: a sign-in token for the Whisper Almanac channel, kept privately on the creator's computer and build service. It is never sold or shared.
  • Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Videos are also subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Questions about this policy: leave a comment on any Whisper Almanac video.
