About Whisper Almanac

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Whisper Almanac is a YouTube channel of long whispered videos to fall asleep to. It is made and published by Spry Labs, and it started in November 2026.

Who is speaking

Every video is narrated by an AI copy of the creator's own whispering voice. The channel never says a video is human-voiced, and every upload is marked on YouTube as altered or synthetic content.

What the videos are

  • Guided manifesting meditations built on Neville Goddard's techniques. Goddard is quoted only from his books whose US copyright was not renewed, never from his lectures or recordings.
  • Money affirmations.
  • Slow, sleepy history and science.

Each new manifesting video plays over a slow painting; the other videos play over dim, calm footage. The videos are for relaxing and falling asleep. They are not medical, financial or mental-health advice, and they promise no result.
